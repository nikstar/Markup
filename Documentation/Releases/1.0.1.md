Markup has a new Liquid Glass icon modeled on the chisel-tip marker in the brush shelf. Its glass ink stroke adapts to the default, dark, clear, and tinted macOS appearances.

Hover over any brush to see its name and keyboard shortcut, such as **Arrow (A)**.

Download **Markup-1.0.1-macOS.zip**, unzip it, and move **Markup.app** to **Applications**. Requires macOS 27 or later on Apple silicon.
