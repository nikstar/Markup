Markup is a standalone AppKit screenshot editor for **macOS 27 or later on Apple silicon**.

Download **Markup-1.0-macOS.zip**, unzip it, and drag **Markup.app** to **Applications**. Open a screenshot from Finder, or leave Markup running and drag the next screenshot thumbnail onto its drop area.

- Draw with Telegram’s pen, arrow, marker, neon, blur, and eraser. Add editable shapes and type captions directly on the screenshot.
- Autosave PNG, JPEG, and TIFF images in place. Reopen an image to continue editing with its local undo history, or press **⌘C** to copy the result.
- Drop images onto the menu-bar pencil, use the Finder service, or enable the bundled Action extension in a compatible macOS app.
